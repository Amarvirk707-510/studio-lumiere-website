About Studio Lumière

Studio Lumière started with a simple idea: the best part of any party is the moment right after the flash goes off, when everyone crowds around the screen waiting for the print. We host every booth ourselves — no self-serve kiosk left running on its own — and we hand guests their prints while they're still warm.

We treat every backdrop like it's going in someone's living room for the next ten years, because for a lot of guests, it will. Based in Fremont, we bring a personally hosted photo booth to weddings, birthdays, baby showers, and company celebrations across the Bay Area.

Founded and run in the Bay Area, one event at a time.
